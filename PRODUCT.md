# Product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (one `index.html`, no build, GitHub Pages)
- **Business:** Gorbachev Pourworks, epoxy flooring. The name is ours (inferred); the client only asked for something with "Gorbachev" and epoxy flooring that is unique. Their own starting idea was "Gorbachev's Flooring".

## Users
- Homeowners who want a garage or basement floor that looks finished and is easy to clean (inferred, the usual epoxy customer).
- Small business owners with workshops, showrooms or shop floors (inferred).
- They arrive on a phone, usually from a Facebook post or message.

## Purpose
Get a visitor to ask for a free quote, by text, with the details the crew needs (which room, which finish, roughly how big, where).

## Positioning (inferred)
A careful, prep-first epoxy crew: the floor looks like poured glass because the boring part (grinding, crack repair) was done properly. Not the cheapest garage-paint job.

## Capabilities
- Floor systems commonly offered by epoxy contractors: flake, metallic, solid colour (from the brief's category note; the client has not confirmed which they offer).
- Rooms: garages, basements, workshops, commercial floors; crack and chip repair (inferred).
- Contact: text message and phone, both to (732) 644-6757 (set 2026-10-20). Email is still a placeholder.

- Calm band style (paper and navy bands, one warm accent as a fill, Cal Sans headings).

- Speaks as "we" (crew size unknown).

## Evidence on hand
- The client's one-line request (in the brief). Nothing else.

## Principles
- Look like a real business's real site, not a sample.
